| # | Claim (source) | Evidence | Verdict | Safer wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | EA + EEGNet + SWA "yields up to 90.97% accuracy on MOABB without calibration" (ProductIdea) | 90.97% is **Subject 1 only**; the 9-subject LOSO mean is **74.31% ± 10.94** (binary) ([note](research/papers/2026-sagayaraju-ea-swa-calibration-bottleneck.md)) | ❌ Misleading | "Published EA + EEGNet pipelines reach ~74% binary accuracy on unseen subjects (9-subject LOSO)" |
| 2 | Alignment needs "the first 3 seconds" of data (QuestionsonArchitecture, ArchitectureDiscussions) | Reference estimation uses **16–32 trials** (Junqueira 2024 used 24); online EMA updates work ([layer-1](research/layer-1-references.md)) | ❌ Unsupported | "Works from the first second with population defaults and adapts silently within ~1–2 minutes of use" (to be measured) |
| 3 | ADHD users get "the exact same accuracy profile" (ArchitectureDiscussions) | EA re-centres the mean covariance; nothing shows equal accuracy for any subgroup | ❌ Unsupported | Remove. At most: "per-user re-centring removes baseline offsets, including atypical ones; subgroup accuracy is untested" |
| 4 | EEGNet has "~3,000 parameters" (ArchitectureDiscussions) | EEGNet-8,2 has 1,716–2,322 parameters ([note](research/papers/2018-lawhern-eegnet.md)) | ✅ Roughly right | "~2,000 parameters" |
| 5 | End-to-end latency < 30 ms (ArchitectureDiscussions) | CNN inference ~2 ms; decision updates every 62.5 ms in a published real-time system (Wimpff 2025) | ✅ Plausible target | Keep as a target; **measure it** |
| 6 | "Synthetic data from GANs = world's largest dataset = our moat" (ProductIdea, Presentation) | Contradicted by the notes' own advice to drop GANs; no evidence that synthetic EEG yields cross-subject gains | ❌ Drop | Moat = consented real-world data flywheel + alignment/adaptation IP + evaluation at scale |
