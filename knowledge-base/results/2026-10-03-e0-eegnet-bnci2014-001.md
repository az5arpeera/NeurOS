# E0: EEGNet on BNCI2014-001, the pipeline check (development numbers)

**Date:** Sat 3 Oct 2026 (the Day-3 plan, Mon 5 – Tue 6 Oct, done early).
**Status:** development numbers; not for the YC application as headline numbers.
**Verdict: ✅ E0 passes.** EEGNet + EA scores **75.0%** against the literature's **74.3% ± 3** (pass band 71.3–77.3%), on our own causal 2-s pipeline.

## The question
Architecture §8.5, E0: *does our pipeline reproduce the literature?*
- **Target:** EA + EEGNet + SWA ≈ 74.3% on BNCI2014-001, left vs right hand, leave-one-subject-out over 9 people ([Sagayaraju 2026](../research/papers/2026-sagayaraju-ea-swa-calibration-bottleneck.md): 74.31% ± 10.94).
- **Go/no-go:** within ±3 points. Otherwise it's a pipeline bug, and work stops until it's found.

## Protocol
- **Data:** BNCI2014-001, all 9 people (none is in the locked test set, D-026), left vs right hand, both sessions: **288 windows per person** (144 per class).
- **Signal, our product contract (§4.2), not the paper's:**
  - causal 4–30 Hz band-pass + notches at 250 Hz, then 128 Hz;
  - **2-s windows** at [cue + 0.5 s, cue + 2.5 s];
  - all 22 EEG channels, reference as recorded.
  - The paper used 1–40 Hz, removed eye artefacts with ICA, and most likely used 4-s windows.
- **Alignment:** EA with **one reference per recording session** (D-029), label-free. Like the paper, the reference uses the whole session (offline). E2 measures the online, growing version.
- **Model:** EEGNet (F1/D/F2 = 16/2/32, 64-sample kernel, dropout 0.5) + 64-d embedding + linear head: **20,002 weights** (D-029).
- **Training, the paper's recipe:**
  - AdamW, learning rate 1e-3 on a cosine to 0, weight decay 0.05, **300 epochs**;
  - **SWA over epochs 226–300**;
  - batches of 8 people × 8 windows.
  - Each fold trains on all 8 other people (no validation people, no early stopping), and each person is tested once.
- **Seed:** one (20261005), for weights, dropout and batch order.
- **Statistics:** balanced accuracy (chance 50%), mean ± SD over people, 95% bootstrap CI over people, paired Wilcoxon over people (§8.3).
- **Compute:** the dev laptop's CPU, 9 folds in parallel (2 threads each): **36 min** with EA, **28 min** without.

## What it means
1. **The pipeline is sound.**
   - We reproduce the paper's number (75.0 vs 74.3) and its pattern: people 2 and 5 hardest, 3 among the best.
   - We did it with the pipeline we ship: causal filters, 2-s windows, no ICA.
   - So harmonisation, labels, epoch timing, filters, EA, the model and the trainer all work together.
   - **The 4-s-window run isn't needed for the verdict.** It was there to tell a bug from a protocol difference if the 2-s run fell short. It's now optional; it would measure what the shorter window costs.
2. **The deep model is worth its complexity:** +8.1 points over the best Riemannian baseline with the same alignment (p = 0.039, better for 7 of 9 people). The Day-2 results set a bar of about 5.
3. **Alignment helps EEGNet too, but less than it helps MDM.** EEGNet gains +2.9 points (better for 8 of 9 people, p = 0.074: suggestive, not significant with 9 people; person 6 loses 4.9), against +12 for MDM. EEGNet's batch norm already absorbs part of each person's scale; EA removes the rest of the person-specific mixing.
4. **An observation, not a claim.**
   - On these 9 people, the zero-calibration model (75.0%) is indistinguishable from a Riemannian model *calibrated* on each person's own first session (75.8%; −0.9 points, p = 0.59). It's equal or better for 5 of 9 people, including the hard ones (2, 4, 5, 6).
   - The comparison flatters us: n = 9, EA here sees each test session in full, and the calibrated baseline is a simple Riemannian model, not a calibrated deep model.
   - Still, it's the first sign of the gap NeurOS is built to close. E2 measures how much of it survives a growing online reference.
5. **Some people stay hard:** 5 of 9 people are below 70%. Calibration doesn't save person 2 either (55.6% within-person). This is "BCI inefficiency", and it's why the headline reports the per-person distribution and the share of people ≥ 70%, not only the mean.

## Caveats
- **One seed.** Deep-network results move by about a point between seeds. 75.0 sits 3.7 points inside the band's lower edge, so the verdict doesn't hinge on the seed. More seeds belong on the cluster (Q6b).
- **9 people:** confidence intervals are ±7 points wide. These numbers check the pipeline; they prove nothing about 380 people.
- **Offline, full-session alignment:** each test session's EA reference uses the whole session (unlabeled). The product gets a growing stream; E2 measures the cost.
- **Development, not headline:** BNCI2014-001 is a literature-comparison dataset used during development (D-026), never part of the locked test set.
